# MCMDS Creative Direction

This file is the MCMDS-specific art-direction layer for MCM Design Co. It translates Brett's saved MCMDS references into reusable visual guidance while preserving the universal system in `DESIGN.md` and the judgment layer in `BRAND.md`.

Use this file when the work should feel specifically MCMDS, Signal, studio-system, technical, artifact-led, evidence-led, nocturnal, interface-adjacent, or visually aligned with Brett's saved MCMDS references.

Do not use this file to override the core system. Role-based typography and casing, the spacing scale, radius rules, containment discipline, accessibility, and channel production requirements still come from the rest of the skill.

## Direction summary

MCMDS should feel like a disciplined design intelligence system: quiet, exacting, technical, tactile, and a little nocturnal. The visual language is built from document grids, measured typography, material evidence, specific imagery, and controlled signal color rather than expressive decoration.

The work should carry the confidence of a printed identity system, the specificity of exposed electronics, and the atmosphere of late-night urban infrastructure. It should feel useful before it feels beautiful.

## Visual thesis

The core idea is controlled signal in a dark field: a rigorous neutral system where information is arranged like evidence, materials are shown through seams and construction details, and color appears only as a precise operational event.

Recognition should come from small, deliberate moves: gridded paper logic, quiet metadata, thin rules, sharp image crops, hard material edges, transparent or exposed layers, and one signal color used with restraint.

## Influence hierarchy

Primary structure: document-grid systems with strict margins, quiet metadata, ruled fields, and calm asymmetric placement.

Secondary language: technical material detail, including transparent layers, ports, screws, labels, bezels, panels, hardware edges, dashboards, and visible internal structure.

Atmosphere: low-light urban infrastructure, black glass, screen glow, night streets, shadowed interiors, and distant illuminated thresholds.

Accent layer: signal color used as status, interruption, charge, heat, route, selection, or alert.

Outliers: do not over-weight literal automotive branding, sci-fi theming, nostalgic office props, or playful hand lettering. Extract useful behavior, not surface identity.

## Guardrails

- Keep sentence case as the default for visible text, including labels and ordinary metadata. Terse technical readouts, coordinates, codes, and artifact-derived labels may use uppercase when casing carries operational meaning; do not extend that exception to ordinary navigation, buttons, or headings.
- Use the role-based type system in `DESIGN.md`: Indivisible for display and brand expression, Inter for product UI and workspace reading, and Departure Mono for genuine technical metadata.
- Use the spacing scale in `DESIGN.md`: `4px`, `8px`, `12px`, `24px`, `48px`, `72px`, `112px`, and `144px`.
- Use the radius system in `DESIGN.md`: `4px`, `6px`, `8px`, and full radius only for compact tags, pills, or status markers.
- Use open spacing, alignment, thin rules, tonal shifts, and type hierarchy before adding boxes or panels.
- Containment is appropriate for repeated objects, modular tools, dense operational panels, physical cards, framed artifacts, tables, or dashboards. It should not become the default page architecture.
- Technical can be warm, tactile, and useful. Avoid generic sci-fi gloss unless the subject explicitly calls for it.
- Dark operational work is allowed to lean technical, but it should still reveal subject matter, material, state, proof, or decision.

## Color

Start from the MCM palette in `DESIGN.md`. The universal accent remains warm orange. This layer adds a controlled secondary signal palette for MCMDS-specific work.

Core palette behavior:

- Use high-contrast neutrals for 80-95% of the surface.
- Use signal color for 5-12% of the surface. For posters, event graphics, or major campaign turns, 15% is the upper limit.
- Choose one active accent family per artifact unless the work is explicitly a data/status system.
- Do not use accent color as a decorative theme or broad wash.

Reference accents:

- Warm orange / red-orange: `#e63e18`, `#ff4b22`, `#ff3b12`, `#ff5a1f`
- Acid lime: `#e7ff2a`
- Screen cyan: `#55d6ff`, `#7be3ff`
- Field green: `#16803c`, `#32d46d`, `#54e68a`
- Signal pink: `#ff3d8d`

Usage:

- Use warm orange for the core MCM signal: emphasis, active state, critical mark, transition rule, alert, motion, heat, or focus.
- Use acid lime for technical status, performance, energy, power, or system-readout emphasis.
- Use cyan for screen glow, cold electronic atmosphere, or ambient light.
- Use green for success, field imagery, research/evidence contexts, or natural/atmospheric counterpoint.
- Use pink only as a contained poster, event, or image-treatment accent. Do not make it a system color.

## Layout and grid

The layout should feel printed and engineered, but not over-boxed. Use a shared alignment system, strong margins, and compact metadata blocks. Let thin rules, spacing, and image placement do most of the architecture.

Preferred structures:

- A large image or field with one to three quiet metadata clusters.
- A document layer placed over a darker backing field.
- Three related objects or notes with one meaningful difference in ruling, image, state, or label.
- A dashboard or operational view where repeated modules are genuinely useful.
- An aperture strip, circular cut, crop, or reveal that exposes another layer beneath.

Asymmetry should feel measured, not casual. Anchor small text at edges, corners, vertical axes, or along a deliberate spine. Dense information should appear in clusters, leaving large regions quiet.

## Typography

Use the typography system in `DESIGN.md`: Indivisible for display and brand expression, Inter for product UI and workspace reading, and Departure Mono for genuine technical metadata. Use tight tracking only for major large display/headline moments; keep normal tracking for body, captions, metadata, labels, tables, buttons, and compact production text.

MCMDS-specific type behavior:

- Keep labels, captions, and ordinary metadata quiet, precise, and sentence case. Uppercase technical readouts remain a narrow exception.
- Use compact annotations, source notes, timestamps, coordinates, and status text as part of the visual language.
- Let one large title, number, value, or image lead the first read when the format needs force.
- Use Departure Mono only when the artifact genuinely needs code, terminal, coordinates, technical notation, identifiers, system readouts, or tabular alignment.
- Do not imitate hand lettering as a system style. Organic marks can appear as rare friction points.

## Imagery and art direction

Imagery should privilege evidence, material, and threshold. Show the actual subject, proof, artifact, product state, place, person, material, or decision.

Good image directions:

- Transparent shells, exposed internals, circuit boards, labels, screws, ports, bezels, seams, disks, LED strips, grilles, and panel breaks.
- Black glass, screen glow, dim interiors, lit portals, brick facades, fire escapes, night streets, and urban infrastructure.
- Document scans, paper texture, gridded notepads, standards sheets, cropped artifacts, and tactile production details.
- Human imagery that is cropped, veiled, obstructed, reflected, or framed through an aperture when that improves the idea.

Avoid generic premium lifestyle photography, stock technology imagery, vague atmospheric filler, sunny brand imagery, soft beige minimalism, and decorative abstract vector art.

## Design elements

Use a restrained technical vocabulary:

- Thin rules, hairlines, quiet dividers, and small registration marks.
- Dot grids, ruled lines, ledger fields, and micro tables.
- Small status dots, glyphs, arrows, counters, coordinates, and timestamps.
- Rectangular panels only where they improve comprehension, repeated structure, tool behavior, or dashboard use.
- Circular apertures, lenses, discs, ports, and registration marks.
- Hard signal bars, LED-like strips, and narrow color blocks.
- Transparent overlays and layer reveals.

If a shape does not measure, reveal, frame, route, signal, group, or clarify, remove it.

## Composition recipes

### Brand identity and stationery

Use black, true white, graphite, and restrained neutral surfaces with one signal intervention. Build from simple documents: cards, sheets, notepads, labels, folders, and standards pages. Use small marks, thin rules, gridded interiors, and tactile paper or screen texture. Let the system feel useful and collectible, not decorative.

### Website and interface

Use the digital product channel rules first. A dark operational base is allowed when it serves the subject. Use shared gutters, semantic structure, the role-based product typography, accessible contrast, and the MCM spacing/radius scale. Use panels only for repeated objects, dense tools, tables, dashboards, or framed artifacts. Use one signal color for active state, progress, alert, or current signal.

### Editorial signal

Start with a strong subject signal: a full-field image, artifact crop, material detail, compact data summary, or precise opening statement. Overlay or place quiet metadata in one to three clusters. Use rotated text only when it behaves like a timestamp, spine label, coordinate, archival notation, or source mark.

### Poster or campaign

Use a true-white, quiet gray, or black field with one signal event: a red-orange glow, acid-lime status block, circular aperture, severe crop, or contained blur. Keep secondary copy small, useful, and edge-aligned. Do not turn the poster into a generic nightlife or sci-fi composition.

### Product or object image

Show one object as evidence. Use overhead, front-on, or macro crops that reveal construction. Highlight transparent plastic, screws, ports, labels, disks, panels, light strips, grilles, shadows, and reflections.

### Deck or document

Use document logic: large margins, small labels, page numbers, thin dividers, clean tables, and occasional image or aperture crops. Section turns may use a large quiet field with one signal color. Content pages should stay readable and structured.

### Image generation

Prompt for specific subject evidence, not abstract mood. Useful language includes: documentary product evidence, transparent casing with visible internals, low-key black glass, small sentence-case metadata labels, thin ruled grid, single red-orange signal mark, acid-lime status accent, visible seams and ports, tactile paper texture, dark urban threshold.

## Quality bar

- The piece reads as designed evidence, not decoration.
- The subject, artifact, material, state, or decision is visible.
- The work uses MCM role-based typography, spacing, radius, and casing discipline.
- Color is limited, purposeful, and under control.
- Technical details feel tactile and useful, not generic sci-fi.
- Containment is justified by repeated objects, tools, tables, framed artifacts, or operational density.
- The composition balances quiet space with compact information.
- Nothing depends on copying a specific logo, product identity, vehicle brand, or proprietary layout.
