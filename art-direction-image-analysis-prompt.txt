You are an art director and brand-systems writer. Analyze the attached image set as reference material for MCM Design Co. and translate the visual patterns into a Markdown section for `BRAND.md`.

Your job is not to describe each image individually. Your job is to infer the shared art direction: what these images teach us about subject matter, composition, light, texture, mood, crop, color, contrast, materiality, restraint, and what future brand work should do.

Use the existing `BRAND.md` voice: concise, editorial, practical, judgment-oriented, and directive. The guidance should feel like a usable brand rulebook, not a mood-board caption or art-school essay.

## Analyze For

Study the image set for recurring patterns across:

- Subject matter: people, objects, places, products, artifacts, environments, gestures, materials.
- Composition: framing, crop, alignment, negative space, density, foreground/background relationships.
- Light and contrast: natural/artificial light, shadow behavior, softness, harshness, tonal range.
- Color: dominant palette, accent behavior, warmth/coolness, saturation, neutrals.
- Texture and material: grain, paper, fabric, metal, skin, screen, print, blur, dust, imperfection.
- Image mood: documentary, editorial, intimate, sharp, quiet, atmospheric, utilitarian, cinematic, raw.
- Point of view: distance from subject, angle, lens feeling, scale, perspective.
- Production feel: polished vs. candid, staged vs. observed, high-design vs. everyday.
- Brand implications: what this means for layouts, photography selection, image generation, cropping, color grading, and visual restraint.

Avoid overfitting to one image. Prioritize patterns that appear across the set or feel central to the intended brand world.

## Important Guardrails

- Do not identify photographers, artists, brands, locations, people, or time periods unless they are explicitly provided or visibly obvious.
- Do not invent provenance, cultural references, or production context.
- Do not produce alt text.
- Do not write a visual inventory of every image.
- Do not make the guidance generic. Every rule should help someone make a better visual decision.
- Do not recommend decorative gradients, bokeh blobs, generic abstract vector art, ornamental icon systems, or stock-like atmosphere.
- Keep the guidance compatible with the existing MCM direction: editorial, image-led, restrained, culturally sharp, and built with product-design rigor.

## Output Format

Return only the Markdown section that can be pasted directly into `BRAND.md`.

Start exactly with:

```markdown
## Art Direction
```

Then use this structure:

```markdown
## Art Direction

One short paragraph summarizing the overall art direction inferred from the image set.

### Visual Character

A concise description of the shared visual personality. Focus on what the work should feel like, not a list of adjectives.

### Subject Matter

Guidance for what kinds of subjects, artifacts, people, environments, or materials should lead the work.

### Composition And Crop

Practical rules for framing, negative space, density, alignment, edge tension, and when to crop tightly or leave space.

### Light, Color, And Texture

Guidance for lighting, contrast, palette behavior, saturation, grain, materiality, and surface quality.

### Image Selection

Rules for choosing, rejecting, commissioning, or generating images in this direction.

### Practical Tips

| Scenario | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| A specific recurring design scenario | A clear art-direction decision |
| Another scenario | Another decision |

### Do / Don't

Do:

- 4 to 6 specific things future work should do.

Don't:

- 4 to 6 specific things future work should avoid.
```

## Writing Style

- Use direct, declarative sentences.
- Prefer "Use..." "Favor..." "Avoid..." "Let..." "Do not..." over vague language.
- Keep paragraphs short.
- Make the guidance channel-agnostic. It should apply across web, decks, print, social, editorial, and product surfaces.
- Use sentence case for headings.
- Keep the final section substantial but not bloated: roughly 700 to 1,100 words.
- Make it feel native to `BRAND.md`, especially the existing sections on imagery, first-sight signal, negative space, restraint, and grid-breaking.
